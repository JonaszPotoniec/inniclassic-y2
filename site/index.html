For the Innioasis Y2

The iPod classic, rebuilt for the click wheel of the Innioasis Y2. Cover Flow, synced lyrics and the menus you remember. Plus what the iPod never had: Bluetooth in the car, Wi-Fi and FM radio.

Download Source code

BetaFree, for personal and educational use. Each release has the firmware and the app.

Bluetooth in the car

Title, artist and a progress bar on the car screen. It pauses when the car turns off, and plays on when you come back.

Synced lyrics

The lyrics follow the song, from .lrc files or from the tags.

Cover Flow that keeps up

The iPod order and layout, smooth on a fast spin, and a letter jump in every list.

Tuned like the iPod

Classic Light and Classic Dark. Each screen is tuned to look and move like the iPod classic.

Wi-Fi that does something

Jellyfin sync, podcasts, upload from a browser, and the correct time from the internet.

FM radio

The tuner of the Y2, with saved stations, from any screen and with the screen off.

The polish

It looks like an iPod. It moves like one.

The user interface is the point. Each screen uses the measurements of the iPod classic: a 30 px status bar, 38 px rows with a drawn chevron, the menu shadow over the album art. Then each screen was tuned on the Y2, frame by frame.

  • The album art next to the menus pans and zooms slowly, as on the iPod.
  • Menus slide in from the right and out to the left. Into Cover Flow, and from a menu with art into Now Playing, the list and the art move apart.
  • Back goes to the row that you came from, at the same height in the list.
  • Menus, questions, notices and waits are iPod screens that the wheel can use, not Android pop-ups.

Two themes

Classic Light. Classic Dark.

Two built-in themes, and every screen, row, icon and colour is tuned for both. One look that works on each screen.

  • Auto: Classic Light by day, Classic Dark after sunset, from your time zone.
  • A theme change applies at once. The music keeps playing, and you stay on the same row.

Cover Flow

Spin through 500 albums. No stutter.

Cover Flow has the positions, reflections and order of the iPod: by artist, then album, without a leading “The”. Each step does much less work than before: in a test of 30 fast wheel steps, the late frames went from 83 to 4.

  • Cover Flow opens at the album that is in Now Playing. With nothing in Now Playing, it opens at a random album.
  • The text under each cover shows the album artist, or “Various Artists” for a compilation.
  • The first Cover Flow after a start is as fast as the next ones: the covers are ready in the background.

Letter jump

4,000 songs? Turn faster.

Turn the wheel fast and the list goes letter by letter, with a large letter box, as on the iPod. It works in all alphabetical lists and in Cover Flow.

Now Playing

The screen you look at most.

An angled cover with a reflection, a glass progress bar and iPod times: 2:08, -0:34, 1:02:08.

  • The song menu: Add to Favorites, Add to On-The-Go, Add to Playlist, Browse Album, Browse Artist.
  • 30 equal volume steps of 2 dB, kept for each output: speaker, headphones, Bluetooth.
  • Fast skips: the new title and cover show about 0.16 s after you push Next (it was 0.56 s).
  • Hold Next or Previous to seek, from 4× up to 120×. Hold Play/Pause to stop.
  • Total Time shows the length of the song in place of the time left.

Lyrics

Sing along.

One push of the centre button shows the lyrics over the full screen. Synced lyrics follow the song, with soft fades at the top and bottom edges.

  • From .lrc files next to the song or from the tags (MP3 USLT, FLAC, ALAC).
  • Settings → Now Playing → Lyrics Keep Screen On: the screen stays on while the lyrics show and the music plays.

Bluetooth

Earbuds, headphones, and the car.

Pair with the wheel only: the Y2 answers each pairing step itself and shows the pairing code on its own screen.

In the car

The car shows the title, artist, album and a progress bar. Its steering-wheel buttons work with the screen off. No access prompts for contacts or messages.

Pause and resume

Turn the car off or take the earbuds out: the music pauses. Connect again within 4 hours, and it plays on at the same place.

Headphones

Unplug wired headphones and the music pauses. The volume is kept for each output.

Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi that does something.

The Y2 has Wi-Fi. Here it plays, syncs and downloads, all from the wheel.

Jellyfin sync

Sign in with QuickConnect, then sync music and audiobooks from your own server.

Podcasts

Subscribe to feeds, stream an episode, or download it for later.

Upload from a browser

Turn on the web server and drag files into a page on your computer. No cable.

Correct time

Sync Time Now sets the clock from the internet, in your time zone.

Last.fm

Scrobbles wait on the Y2 and go to Last.fm when Wi-Fi is on. Sign in with a QR code.

Updates

Settings → General → System Update gets the newest release from GitHub and installs it.

FM Radio

Radio, the iPod nano way.

Main menu → FM Radio opens the tuner and turns the radio on, on the last station. Push Menu for the Radio menu.

  • While the radio plays, Play/Pause pauses it and Next/Previous go to the next saved station, from any screen.
  • One sound at a time: music starts, the radio stops; the radio starts, the music pauses.
  • It plays on with the screen off.

Your library

All your music, the iPod way.

Artists, Albums, Compilations, Songs, Genres, Composers, Playlists and Folders. Hide or reorder the items you do not use. Shuffle Songs plays the whole library.

  • MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG Vorbis, Opus, ALAC and AAC, on the internal storage and the microSD card.
  • A play counts after half the song (or 4 minutes), for Top 25 and for .scrobbler.log (Last.fm, ListenBrainz).
  • Audiobooks with a bookmark for each book, and videos.

Details

Small things, done right.

  • Settings in iPod groups (Playback, Display, Now Playing, Status Bar…) with On/Off switches. Export and import all settings as one file.
  • Each push does one action, on release. One vibration for each step; the click has its own volume.
  • Notices in a small box, and waits with the iPod progress bar or spinner, not Android toasts and dialogs.
  • Hold the Power button: a Power screen (Restart, Power Off) that the wheel can use.
  • On the Y2, the battery level comes from the battery voltage, not from the Y2's own gauge, which can show 69 % twenty minutes before the Y2 turns off. Settings → Status Bar → Battery Percentage puts the number in the battery icon.
  • A “Battery low” banner at 10 % and an iPod Please charge screen at 3 %. When the battery is empty, the Y2 pauses the music, saves the play queue and turns itself off.
  • Connect the USB cable and choose Connect as Storage: the Y2 shows on your computer as a disk.

Install

Try it on your Y2.

The download is the latest release on GitHub: the firmware zip for each player (rom_y2.zip for the Y2), the APK and the checksums. The release also has the firmware for the Innioasis Y1: see the README.

Firmware

  1. Open the Inniclassic page on innioasis.app and install Innioasis Updater CE (the page links to it).
  2. In Updater CE, choose Y2 as the device model and Inniclassic as the software. Select a release and click Install / Restore.
  3. Power off the Y2 and connect it only when the updater asks. When the Y2 restarts, it starts in the main menu.

Manual install: download rom_y2.zip from the latest release, rename it to rom.zip, and choose it in the updater as the local firmware package.

A full flash erases the internal storage. Music on the microSD card stays. Keep the cable in until the update is done.

App only (APK)

On a Y2 that already runs this firmware (ADB is on), connect the USB cable:

adb install -r InniClassic-<version>.apk

The update keeps your settings, music, playlists and .scrobbler.log. The firmware installs the app as a system app, so adb uninstall cannot remove it: for an APK with a different signing key, flash the firmware of that release.

Build it yourself

JDK 17 and the Android SDK:

git clone 
cd 
./gradlew assembleDebug
